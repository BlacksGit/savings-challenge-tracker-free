# Free savings challenge ideas: 52-week, reverse 52-week and $5 bill

Totals below are sums of the amounts shown. Pick the challenge that fits your income.

## 52-week challenge

Save $1 in week 1, $2 in week 2, and so on to $52 in week 52. Total saved: $1,378. The last weeks are the hardest.

## Reverse 52-week challenge

Start at $52 in week 1 and go down to $1 in week 52. Same $1,378 total, but the big amounts come while motivation is high and the holiday months are cheaper.

## $1,000 in 50 weeks

Save $20 a week. Easy to remember and to automate with a weekly transfer.

## $5 bill challenge

Every $5 bill that comes into your wallet goes into a jar. 100 bills is $500. Works best when you use cash.

## No-spend days

Pick a number of days each month where you spend nothing beyond bills. Mark them on a calendar and add up what you saved.

## Sinking funds

Save a fixed amount monthly for known costs (car repair, gifts, vacation, annual fees) so they are never a surprise. Goal divided by months left equals your monthly amount.

## Tips that help

Move the money to a separate savings account the day you get paid. Pick the challenge that fits your income, not the one that looks best. Miss a week? Do two boxes the next week instead of quitting.
